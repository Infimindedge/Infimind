# Content and launch notes

## Testimonials

All demo and sample testimonial records are unpublished. The public carousel
uses its honest empty state until genuine, permission-cleared family
testimonials are supplied.

## Global presence stories

Sample family quotes have been removed. Locations display only their approved
city, country, and flag information.

## Success Stories

The Success Stories page is planned for a later phase and is not linked from
public navigation.

## Admin

The legacy admin implementation remains in the project for future development,
but `/admin` deliberately fails closed in the static launch. Content changes
should be made in source files, reviewed in Git, and deployed through the
approved branch.

## Enquiries

The public forms require a deployed Google Apps Script Web App URL. Follow
`GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md` and complete the end-to-end Sheet test before launch.

## Legal review

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service still require review against the actual
Infimind legal entity, governing law, operating countries, retention practices,
and third-party services before they are treated as final legal advice.

## Photography

Confirm Infimind holds the rights to use and redistribute every supplied image.
Asset provenance notes are recorded in `ASSET_CREDITS.md`.
